thumb letters a e m n s t look alike to a camera, because the thumb hides behind the fingers. Face your palm to the screen, keep the thumb tip visible, and if one keeps failing, pick it here and press teach while you hold it. It learns your hand.
This covers fingerspelling only: the ASL alphabet, letter by letter. Real sign language is a full language with its own signs and grammar, and this is not a replacement for it or for an interpreter. Pictures are flipped to match the way you see yourself on screen.

sign/speller

Spell words with your hand using the ASL alphabet. Hold each letter still to type it. A picture of every letter stays on screen, so you can learn while you spell, then have it read out loud.

Fingerspelling only, not full sign language. Nothing leaves your device.
